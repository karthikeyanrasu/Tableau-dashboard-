# NYC High School Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The NYC High School Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Tableau project analyzing the performance of New York City public high schools across cohort years 2014–2019.

The dashboard focuses on key indicators including graduation rates, dropout rates, diploma distribution, and national high school ranking. The goal of the project is to transform school performance data into interactive visualizations that make it easier to identify performance patterns across schools, boroughs, genders, student types, and cohort years.

## Objectives

- Analyze graduation and dropout patterns across NYC public high schools.
- Compare school performance across boroughs and demographic groups.
- Examine diploma distribution across different diploma types.
- Analyze changes in NYC's national high school ranking across the studied cohort years.
- Build an interactive Tableau dashboard for exploring school performance data.
- Identify schools and groups with notable performance differences.

## Dataset

The analysis covers:

- **400+ NYC public high schools**
- **Cohort years:** 2014–2019
- Graduation rate
- Dropout rate
- Diploma distribution
- Gender
- Borough
- Student type
- National high school ranking

The Tableau dashboard provides interactive views of school performance using filters and coordinated visualizations.

Key areas of analysis include:

### Graduation Performance
Analyzes graduation rates across schools and cohort years and identifies schools with lower graduation performance.

### Dropout Analysis
Examines dropout rates by gender and borough to identify differences in student outcomes.

### Diploma Distribution
Analyzes the distribution of diploma types, including Normal Regents and Advanced Regents diplomas.

### National Ranking
Tracks changes in NYC's national high school ranking across the cohort years included in the dataset.

## Key Findings

- **52 schools (13%)** had graduation rates below 60%.
- The male dropout rate was **11%**, compared with **7% for females**.
- Advanced Regents diplomas were attained **65% less often** than Normal Regents diplomas.
- NYC's national high school ranking changed from **Rank 3 in 2014–15 to Rank 10 in 2019**.
- The analysis also identified differences in dropout performance across boroughs.
